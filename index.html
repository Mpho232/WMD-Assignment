Our Story

Made with Passion

M.R Foods was born right next to Botswana Accountancy College — built for the students who need real food, fast. We know BAC life: tight schedules, long lectures, and not enough time for a proper meal.

That's why everything on our menu is freshly prepared, affordably priced, and ready in minutes. From our signature crispy burgers to loaded quesadillas, every bite is made with care.
